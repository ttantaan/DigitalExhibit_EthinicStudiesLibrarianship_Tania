The books

An annotated list of ten contemporary Gothic works, published 2023 to the present.

Audiobook

  • In Your Heart a Devil Vanessa Santos and Barrie Kreinik

    A short story about the experience of immigration. It follows a Portuguese woman navigating displacement and alienation. Her experience of immigration is highlighted by loneliness and vulnerability in a hostile Scottish environment. It contains elements of Scottish folklore.

    Focus: Scottish rural landscape

    Blackstone Audio, 2026. BN ID: 2940201793272

Physical books

  • The Unworthy: A Novel Agustina María Bazterrica, translated by Sarah Moses

    Through religious terror, the story examines oppressive institutions from postcolonial and Global South perspectives. In a world of social ruin and environmental collapse, gendered power is used to address internalized misogyny.

    Focus: Latin America, with Argentine influence from the author

    Scribner, an imprint of Simon & Schuster, 2025. ISBN: 9781668051887

  • The Possession of Alba Díaz Isabel Cañas

    Uses possession and supernatural conflict to confront continuing conflicts of colonial power. Through Catholicism, the story examines how imposed religious traditions can become both a colonial influence and a means of cultural resistance.

    Focus: Postcolonial Mexico

    Berkley, an imprint of Penguin Random House LLC, 2026. ISBN: 9780593641088

  • Vampires of El Norte Isabel Cañas

    Uses supernatural conflict within the Mexican-American War to expose violence committed by Anglo-American imperialism. Vampires serve as a symbol of intergenerational trauma caused by war, displacement, and colonial aggression.

    Focus: Early Republic Mexico

    Penguin Publishing Group, 2024. ISBN: 9780593436738

  • The Reformatory: A Novel Tananarive Due

    Set during the Jim Crow era, the novel frames its historical setting as the ground for supernatural violence and racial oppression. Through spirits rooted in Southern Black traditions, supernatural entities and practices represent cultural memory and resistance rather than superstition. Race is used to highlight historical and intergenerational trauma.

    Focus: Black Americans

    Saga Press, an imprint of Simon & Schuster, LLC, 2025. ISBN: 9781982188351

  • Dazzling Chịkọdịlị Emelụmadụ

    An analysis of postcolonial education that explores the effect of colonial Christianity. Igbo cosmology presents the supernatural as a grounding form of knowledge and knowing rather than superstition. It also challenges the Western use of the Other by centering a homegrown supernatural tradition.

    Focus: Nigerian Igbo communities

    The Overlook Press, 2023. ISBN: 9781419769795

  • Bad Cree: A Novel Jessica Johns

    Indigenous ways of knowing are explored through dreams, drawing on Indigenous and matriarchal storytelling traditions. Using Gothic and horror elements, the story explores the aftermath of loss and colonialism.

    Focus: Native Americans, Cree

    Doubleday, 2023. ISBN: 9780593467947

  • God-Disease An Chang Joon

    A collection of nine short stories exploring diasporic identity and the shifting boundary between American and Korean identities. Set in a Korean Southern Gothic tradition, the stories also explore the fragility of language.

    Focus: Korean American

    Sarabande Books, 2025. ISBN: 9781956046335

  • The Creation of Half-Broken People: A Novel Siphiwe Gloria Ndlovu

    In a setting between museums and exploitation in Africa, a woman is plagued with visions. Shaped by postcolonial social hierarchy, she explores the complexity of colonial history as a descendant of Captain John Good of King Solomon’s Mines .

    Focus: Zimbabwe’s colonial history

    Anansi International, 2025. ISBN: 9781487013271

  • She Is a Haunting Trang Thanh Tran

    Set in a French colonial house with elements of the supernatural, the story explores ancestral ties and assimilation through a living ghost. It examines the conservative expectations placed on the Asian diaspora, along with generational guilt.

    Focus: Vietnam, French colonial period

    Bloomsbury YA, Bloomsbury Publishing Inc, 2024. ISBN: 9781547615681
